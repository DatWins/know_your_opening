Every game here starts with **1.Nc3**, and the first reply from Black is already on the board. You play White and type the move you think White played, one move at a time. After each of your moves, Black's reply appears on the scoresheet.

Each game has a par of **100 points**, shared evenly over the moves you guess. Each move's par is shown on the scoresheet:

- Right on the first try earns that move's full par.
- Each wrong guess takes one point off.
- **Hint** shows the board for that move. It doesn't use up a try, but one point comes off the move's score when you find it.
- When the tries run out, the move is shown and you score 0 for it. **Show me** does the same at once.
- A move that isn't legal in the position costs nothing.

Try to play with the board hidden and keep the position in your head. If that is too hard at first, switch on "Show the board as I play".

The games are grouped in the menu below: **How You Win**, **How You Lose**, **Traps White Set**, **Traps against White**, **Middle Game**, and **Endgame**.
